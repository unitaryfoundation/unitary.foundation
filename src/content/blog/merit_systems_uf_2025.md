## Intro - the problem
OS powers all of the the great tools we rely on, but people mostly do it on a volunteer basis, unpaid, which makes it vulnerable to a being abandoned by developers -- and it restricts the type of people who can contribute to OS in the first place to those who are typically more financially stable with a reasonable amount of free time on their hands. 

Our mission at Unitary Foundation is to help grow a quantum OS ecosysytem that benefits the most people, and we are dedicated to compensating and recognizing individuals for their work in this field. We have our longest running microgrant program of $4k no-strings attached money to work on a quantum OS project, and our annual unitaryHACK hackathon[link] (with record participation this year at [X] number of projects/issues/dollars/people). The bounty system for unitaryHACK is great for incentivizing new contributors over a short time period, and we wanted to extend that into the rest of the year.

## Introducing UF x Merit
That's why we are partnering with Merit Systems[link], a payment service platform specifically designed for open-source contributors. It interfaces with your GitHub account and makes the process of getting paid straightforward in a wide array of countries.
