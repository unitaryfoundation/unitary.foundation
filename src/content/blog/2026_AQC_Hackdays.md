In 2024, Unitary Foundation began the HACKdays Program as part of unitaryHACK. While the majority of unitaryHACK takes place digitally, our aim for this program is to foster local, in-person meetups at colleges and companies around the world. By partnering with students, professors, and quantum enthusiasts globally, **we're able to empower local organizers to bring UF's mission of "making quantum tech accessible to the most people" to their communities.**

Today, we're highlighting a dedicated national effort from our colleagues at the **African Quantum Consortium**. Thank you to the AQC leadership and the chapter leaders for joining us this year. We're looking forward to your events!
