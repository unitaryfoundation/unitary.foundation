Since 2024, Unitary Foundation has been hosting the HACKdays Program during each year's unitaryHACK. While the majority of unitaryHACK takes place digitally, we're aiming to foster local, in-person meetups at local colleges and companies. By partnering with students, professors, and quantum enthusiasts around the world, we're able to empower local organizers to bring UF's mission of "making quantum tech accessible to the most people" to their communities. 

For the full list of HACKday events, check out the [unitaryHACK homepage](https://unitaryhack.dev/). Today, we're highlighting a dedicated national effort from our colleagues at the African Quantum Consortium. Thank you to the AQC leadership and the chapter leads for joining us this year. We're looking forward to your events!
