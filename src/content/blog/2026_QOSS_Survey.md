The wait is over: the latest Quantum Open Source Software survey is now open! Our annual survey is back to give everyone in quantum tech a voice to **weigh in and build a clearer, more representative view of where the field and community stands.** The survey explores topics ranging from community demographics to research focus, tools, and platforms.

Whether you build or use quantum software in any capacity, we’d love for you to take ~20 minutes to complete the survey. **This year's QOSS survey will be open a bit longer: through Thursday, October 29, 2026.** Thank you for helping us capture this snapshot!

Ready to take the survey? [Click here to get started!](https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/QOSSSurvey26)
<a href="https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/QOSSSurvey26" target="_blank"><img src="/images/2026_QOSS.png"></a>

Like every year, to serve as a helpful asset for the entire ecosystem, all anonymized data will be made public to help everyone better understand the community's needs. Unitary Foundation will synthesize the data, highlight key insights, and publish the full summary on our website later this year.

A huge thank you to the Unitary Foundation members, advisors, and partners who helped build, test, and refine this survey to ensure it remains a valuable community asset.

[**Fill out the QOSS Survey**](https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/QOSSSurvey26)
