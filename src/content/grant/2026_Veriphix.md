To **Bo Yang, Elham Kashefi, and Harold Ollivier** for **Secure Observable Estimation and Error Mitigation in Veriphix**. The project will pull from practical cryptographic verification protocols for near-term quantum applications and error mitigation that was developed by a cross-institutional research team from Inria, ENS, and the University of Tokyo. These protocols will be implemented into their project, Veriphix.
