To **Aleks Kissinger** to develop **ZX Calculus Online** with the support of **Nicolas De Matteis** and **Harry Stoltz**. This project will build an online, gamified introduction to quantum computation using the graphical language of the ZX calculus. This site will operate in two modes: a "Duolingo"-like mode, where users will be able to learn quantum in small doses by solving a curated series of ZX-calculus based puzzles, and a "sandbox" mode, where users can create, share, and permalink ZX-calculus proofs.

**Project Champions:** Klem Jankiewicz, Leonardo Disilvestro, Bradley Chase
